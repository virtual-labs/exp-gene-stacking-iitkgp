Instructions
  1. Click on the button "Select target plant" to display target plant.
  2. Click on the button "Isolate/synthesise target genes" to display gene.
  3. Click on the button "Subclone genes into suitable plant expression vector" to display the recombinant vector.
  4. Click on the button "Use recombinant vector with genes to transform Agrobacterium tumefaciens" to display Transformed tumefaciens cell.
  5. Now click on the target plant image to display Plant callus.
  6. Click on the button "Infect plant callus with transformed Agrobacterium tumefaciens harbouring the Ti – plasmid based recombinant plant expression vector" to display the plant callus with Agrobacterium tumefaciens colonies.
  7. Click on the button "Regenerate plant under antibiotic selection pressure from infected callus" to display gene stacked plant T0.
  8. Click on the button "Isolate DNA/RNA from T0 gene stacked plant" to display the DNA and RNA.
  9. Click on the button "DNA and RNA" image to display the thermal cycler.
  10. Click on the button "thermal cycler" image to display Agarose gel with DNA/RNA bands.
  11. Click on the button "DNA and RNA bands" image to display the "Western Blot apparatus" image .
  12. Click on the button "Western Blot apparatus" image for checking of protein expression. An Autoradiograph with confirmatory protein bands image will appear.
  13. Now, click on Gene stacked plant T0 image. This will collect the seed and plant seed image will appear.
  14. Click on the seeds image to grow further generations of the plant (T1, T2, etc).
  15. Click on the button "Molecular re-confirmation of T1, T2, etc plants by PCR/qPCR and protein expression using Western Blot" to show DNA/RNA and protein bands.
  16. click on the button "Evaluation of the introduced/improved traits in the gene stacked plants", to display the improved traits of plants.
